A little about me

PEOPLE FIRST. BRAMPTON FIRST. TIME FOR CHANGE.

“There is a time for everything, and a season for every purpose.”

Hello Brampton

My name is Sameer Tandon — although my family and friends simply call me Sam. I am running for Regional Councillor for Brampton Wards 2 & 6.

I want you to know a little about the person behind the name.

I lost my father in a tragic road accident when I was 21. That experience taught me resilience, responsibility and the importance of family.

With my mother’s blessings, I completed my B.Com. and MBA. I have lived and built my life here for more than a decade, while building a career spanning decades in administration, Finance and serving people. Over the years, I have had the opportunity to work with leading Canada organizations, including The Salvation Army, where serving and supporting people in our communities has been an important part of my journey.

For many years, I have also volunteered and helped people wherever I could. I believe community service should not begin a few months before an election — helping people should be part of who you are.

I am blessed with my wife, my son and our beloved cat Jack, who is truly another child in our family.

What I am hearing across Wards 2 & 6

  • 🏠 Overcrowded and poorly maintained properties
  • 🚗 Parking and too many vehicles on residential streets
  • 🗑️ Garbage, dumping and neighbourhood cleanliness
  • 🌱 Property-standard issues
  • ❄️ Snow and sidewalk concerns
  • 📞 Complaints that seem to go unanswered
  • 💰 Lower property taxes and responsible spending
  • 👵 Seniors’ dignity, respect and better support

I want to be present in Ward 2 & 6 every week

  • Regular community meetings in Ward 2 & 6 neighbourhoods
  • A direct resident community group for local concerns and updates
  • Regular neighbourhood walks so I can see problems firsthand
  • Better communication so residents know what is happening with their concerns

Our current Regional Councillor has served three terms. I respect anyone who serves our community, but I also believe residents deserve choice, accountability and the opportunity for change.

This time, I am asking Ward 2 & 6 residents to give me an opportunity.

Give me the chance to serve — and then judge me by my accessibility, my effort and my results.

BRAMPTON FIRST.

Sameer Tandon

Our Platform

My Priorities for Wards 2 & 6

Affordable Taxes

Protecting your hard-earned money.

I will champion responsible budgeting, eliminate wasteful spending, and ensure every tax dollar delivers real value for residents while keeping Regional property taxes affordable.

Affordable Housing

Helping families find a place to call home.

I will support practical solutions to increase affordable housing, reduce homelessness, and work with all levels of government to build complete, livable communities.

Safe Communities

Safety is not a luxury — it is a right.

I will support strong police services, reliable paramedic response, emergency preparedness, and community safety initiatives that help keep every neighbourhood safe.

Better Health & Seniors

Healthier communities. Better care.

I will strengthen Peel Public Health programs, improve access to mental health services, support paramedics, and ensure seniors receive the dignity, care, and respect they deserve.

Strong Local Economy

Supporting businesses. Creating opportunity.

I will advocate for reducing unnecessary red tape, supporting entrepreneurs, attracting investment, and creating an environment where businesses can grow and create good-paying jobs.

Opportunities for Youth

Investing in the next generation.

I will support youth employment, skills development, recreation, leadership opportunities, and partnerships that help young people succeed and reach their full potential.

Better Transportation

Moving people safely and efficiently.

I will advocate for better regional transit, safer roads, improved traffic flow, and transportation planning that supports families, workers, seniors, students, and local businesses.

Transparency

Nothing hidden. Nothing wasted.

I will champion open decision-making, clear budgets, responsible spending, and regular communication with residents so every major decision and every tax dollar is accountable to the people of Peel.

My Commitment to You

Affordable taxes. Safe communities. Affordable housing. Better health. Strong businesses. Opportunities for youth. Better transportation. Transparency.

Together, we will build a stronger, safer, healthier, and more affordable Wards 2 & 6.

The Roadmap

My Action Plan

💰

1. Lower Property Taxes & Cut Waste

I will push to cut wasteful spending first, review unnecessary costs and use those savings to help lower property-tax pressure while protecting essential services.

🛡️

2. Safer Streets & Safer Neighbourhoods

I will push for stronger enforcement, quicker action on repeat problems, cleaner neighbourhoods and practical measures to improve road and community safety.

🚌

3. Better Transit & Less Traffic

I will focus on more reliable transit, reducing overcrowding and delays, and improving traffic flow based on where residents actually need service.

👵

4. Dignity & Better Support for Seniors

I will advocate for reliable transportation, accessible services, affordable housing options and stronger support so seniors can live with dignity and independence.

🏥

5. Better Healthcare for Brampton

I will keep pressure on all levels of government to move Brampton’s healthcare projects forward, while demanding clear timelines and accountability for public funding.

🏠

6. Affordable Housing, Good Jobs & Opportunities

I will support more affordable housing, help small businesses grow, reduce unnecessary municipal red tape and work to attract more jobs and investment to Brampton.

My Commitment

Spend responsibly. Listen to residents. Measure results. Be accountable.
